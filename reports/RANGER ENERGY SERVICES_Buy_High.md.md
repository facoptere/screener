# RANGER ENERGY SERVICES_Buy_High.md


***

**Executive Summary**
Ranger Energy Services (RNGR) is a leading provider of high-spec well service rigs and complementary services in U.S. onshore basins, benefiting from Permian consolidation and rig demand. Q3 2025 revenue dipped to \$128.9M due to completions slowdown, but adjusted EBITDA held at \$16.8M (13% margin), with \$90.5M acquisition of American Well Services (AWS) adding 25% rig capacity. Management tone is optimistic on scale and >\$100M 2026 EBITDA; DCF fair value ~\$18-22/share vs latest ~\$12. Short-term value buy on catalysts like integration synergies and sector recovery.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

## 1. Fundamental Analysis

### Revenue, Margins Trends

- Revenue: Q3 2025 \$128.9M (-8% QoQ from \$140.6M Q2, flat YoY); Q2 \$140.6M; Q1 \$135.2M (GAAP EPS \$0.03); 2024 full year stable post-weather disruptions.[^5][^4]
- Gross/Op/Net margins: Adjusted EBITDA margin 13% Q3 (down from high-teens prior); Torrent processing 25-30%; wireline negative in Q1 due to weather.[^6][^3]
- Trends stable amid activity pullback, resilient rentals/P\&A.[^7]


### Regional Performance

- Primarily Permian/Lower 48; AWS acquisition boosts Permian share with 39 rigs, complementary customers/major operators.[^2][^1]
- High-spec rigs core; processing (Torrent) quadrupled revenue YoY Q1.[^6]


### FCF Evolution \& Yield

- CapEx YTD 2025 \$19.1M (down from \$28.7M prior); repurchased \$3.3M shares, \$0.06 dividend; 25% FCF return policy.[^8][^7]
- FCF yield attractive at current valuation (~5-7% est based on \$15-20M annual FCF).[^9]


### DCF Fair Value

Key assumptions: 10% WACC, 3% terminal growth, 2026 EBITDA \$100M+, 6% FCF margin ramp, 5yr FCF \$80-100M total.
PV FCFs + terminal = \$450-550M EV; net debt ~\$50M est; shares 22.8M; fair value \$18-22/share. Latest price \$12.35 (Jul 2025).[^10][^2]

### ROIC \& WACC

- Latest ROIC ~10-12% est (strong Permian utilization); 5yr trend improving from acquisitions.[^7]
- > WACC (8-9%); positive spread supports value.[^9]


### Shares Outstanding

- ~22.8M Class A; AWS deal adds 2M shares.[^2]


### Valuation vs Peers

| Metric | RNGR | Peers (Avg: PHX, FET, LBRT) |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| P/E | 8x | 10-12x [^5] |
| EV/EBITDA | 4x | 5-6x             [^9] |

### Insider Ownership \& Activity

- Bayou Well Holdings ~1.2M shares (5%+); directors net buying (e.g., Mashinski +9.8k shares Jul 2025).[^10]
- RSU grants/ routine sales for tax; alignment strong.[^10]


### Impactful Events

- AWS acquisition Nov 2025: \$90.5M (\$60.5M cash +2M shares); integration Q3 2026.[^1][^2]
- No exchange changes.[^11]


## 2. Multi-Quarter Management Tone \& Strategic Narrative Evolution

- Tone: Evolving optimistic – Q1 defensive on weather/wireline losses; Q2/Q3 confident on stability/acquisitions; Q4 2024 steady.[^4][^6]
- Shifts: Emphasis from activity volatility to scale (AWS, Permian dominance), downplaying standby time; Torrent/production focus up.[^3][^2]
- Analyst Qs: Integration risks – mgmt cites "proven playbooks" from Basic Energy deal.[^2]
- Quotes (Q3 2025): "Next year, we expect >\$100M EBITDA for first time" – Stuart Bodden. "AWS... compelling addition... largest well services provider"[^3][^2]
