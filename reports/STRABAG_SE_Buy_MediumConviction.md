## Executive Summary (<150 words)

STRABAG SE delivered record 2025 results (output €20.4bn +6%, EBIT margin 6.7%, net income €916m +11%) and entered 2026 with a €33.1bn order backlog (+18% YoY) despite weather headwinds. The company trades at deeply discounted multiples (TTM P/E 10.9x, EV/EBITDA ~4.4x) versus European construction peers (Vinci ~6.7x EV/EBITDA, Hochtief higher) while boasting a fortress balance sheet (net cash ~€3.5bn+), high-single-digit FCF yield, and visible growth from Strategy 2030 execution (Australia entry, water infrastructure). 2026 guidance (output ~€22bn, EBIT margin 5.0–5.5%) appears conservative. DCF and peer-multiple implied fair value €120–160/share. Strong backlog conversion, margin resilience, and re-rating potential support a **Buy** for 12–24 month horizon with attractive risk/reward and 3.3%+ dividend yield.

## 4. Sector and Macro View

**Sector Overview (4–6 lines)**: European construction & engineering remains in a **structural upcycle** driven by EU Green Deal, energy transition, mobility infrastructure renewal, and data-centre/housing needs. Pricing power is moderate-to-good for large integrated players with backlog; consolidation ongoing (acquisitions in water/specialist niches). Margins recovering post-inflation but sensitive to public budget timing and labour availability. STRABAG’s integrated model (construction + materials + services) provides relative resilience.

**Geopolitical Sensitivities**: Moderate. Energy/asphalt/bitumen costs exposed to oil prices or supply disruptions (e.g., Strait of Hormuz scenario). Company uses price-escalation clauses and local/long-term procurement. Diversified geography reduces single-country risk. No material direct Russia exposure post-sanctions adjustments.

**Key Macro Sensitivities**:
- Positive: Lower interest rates (stimulates private building), EU recovery funds/infra spending, energy transition capex.
- Negative: Sharp recession (delays public projects), sustained high rates or inflation spikes in materials/labour.
- Currency: Primarily EUR exposure; limited USD impact.

**Competitive Positioning & Moat**: **Stable-to-widening**. Scale (#5 European construction by revenue historically), vertical integration, strong regional density in core DACH/CEE markets, and growing international/specialist capabilities (tunnelling, water, offshore) create switching costs and bidding advantages on complex projects. Strategy 2030 expansions (Australia, water) are extending the moat into higher-growth adjacencies. Not eroding.
