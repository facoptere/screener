# PC PARTNERS GROUP_Buy_High.md

PC Partner Group Limited (HKEX:1263, SGX:PCT) is a designer, manufacturer, and trader of VGA cards (own brands ZOTAC, Manli, ASK), EMS services, and PC components, with manufacturing in China and Indonesia, sales globally. Revenue grew 10% to HK\$10,082M in FY2024 from HK\$9,167M in FY2023, driven by own-brand VGA cards (RTX series); H1 FY2025 revenue up 28.5% to HK\$6,355M. Profit attributable to owners doubled to HK\$262M in FY2024; H1 FY2025 profit HK\$250M. Strong FCF HK\$135M FY2024; net cash position. Trading at ~HK\$1.48 (Jan 2026), undervalued vs peers on EV/EBITDA ~4x; delisting HKEX for SGX primary listing catalyst.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]

## 1. Fundamental Analysis

### Revenue, Margins Trends

- Revenue: FY2022 HK\$10,775M → FY2023 HK\$9,167M (-15%) → FY2024 HK\$10,082M (+10%); H1:2025 HK\$6,355M (+28.5% YoY).[^7][^1]
- Gross margin: FY2023 7.6% → FY2024 9.5%; H1:2025 10.5% (vs 11.3% H1:2024) – higher RTX50 GPU costs offset volume.[^1]
- Op margin: FY2023 1.4% → FY2024 3.0%; net margin FY2023 0.7% → FY2024 2.6%.[^7]
- VGA cards 57% revenue FY2024 (own-brand 49%, up 62% H1:2025).[^1]

## 4. Sector \& Macro View

PC hardware sector mid-cycle upturn: GPU demand from AI/gaming; pricing power on premium RTX (NVIDIA dependency risk). Consolidation via EMS outsourcing; ZOTAC brand strengthens.[^14]

Macro sensitivities:

- Trade/tariffs (US-PRC): Adverse (VGA exports); hedge via Indonesia.
- Rates: Neutral (net cash).
- FX: USD strength aids (80% rev); RMB weak hurts costs.
- Energy: Minor (mfg).

Competitive moat: Widening – ZOTAC \#1 mini-GPU share; vertical integration Dongguan→Batam; 30yr IP. Vs Quanta/Foxconn: Niche gaming edge.[^2]
