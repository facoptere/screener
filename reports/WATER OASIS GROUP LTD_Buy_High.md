# WATER OASIS GROUP LTD_Buy_High.md

**Executive Summary**
Water Oasis Group Ltd (1161.HK), a leading HK beauty services/retail operator (Oasis, Glycel), delivered FY25 revenue of HK\$981M (flat YoY), PAT HK\$81M (8.3% margin, +19% YoY), and H1FY26 NP HK\$55M (+39%). Debt-free with HK\$747M cash (HK\$1.10/share), FCF ~HK\$112M (18% yield), ROIC ~16% >> WACC ~9%. DCF fair value HK\$3.51 (vs current HK\$0.93 Jan 2026, 277% upside). Insider ownership 46%, recent buys. **Buy**: Cash fortress + service moat = asymmetric value <2yr.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5]
