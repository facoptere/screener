## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**TSUGAMI CORP** (TSE: 6101) is a Tokyo-listed manufacturer of precision machine tools and measuring equipment, with commanding competitive position in CNC automatic lathes and multi-tasking turning centers. The company trades at a **significant discount to intrinsic value**, with management executing a turnaround narrative after a period of margin compression. H1 FY2025 results (ended Sept 30, 2025) demonstrate accelerating momentum: revenue +20.6% YoY to ¥60B, operating profit +53.4% to ¥15.2B, and net income +48.4% to ¥7.1B. Management revised FY2026 guidance upward to ¥115B revenue (7.1% growth) and ¥27B operating profit (15.8% growth), suggesting operational leverage is normalizing. At ¥1,890 per share, valuation is compelling: P/E of 8.2x vs. industry average 12-13x, DCF fair value of ¥2,779-6,643 across reasonable scenarios. Primary catalyst is China demand recovery and capacity expansion completion (Japan Nagaoka facility in FY2027, India assembly in FY2025). Key risks include macroeconomic slowdown in China (84% of revenue), geopolitical tensions, and execution on capex plans. **Rating: BUY** | **Confidence: High** | **Target: ¥2,500-3,200 (6-18 months)**.

## 4. SECTOR & MACRO VIEW

### 4.1 Precision Machinery Sector Overview

**Industry Stage**: Mid-cycle expansion in demand, following post-pandemic normalization (2023) and demand rebound (2024-present)

**Market Drivers**:
- **Semiconductor Manufacturing Equipment**: Global wafer fab expansion drives machine tool orders; TSUGAMI benefits from precision lathe demand for component sub-assembly
- **Electric Vehicle Supply Chain**: EV production ramping creates demand for battery component machining; automotive customer base shifting from ICE to EV introduces temporary disruption but longer-term growth
- **Aerospace & Defense**: Post-COVID recovery; military spending increase in developed markets supports high-precision machinery demand
- **General Automation Trend**: Industry 4.0, robotics integration, AI-driven manufacturing increasing adoption of CNC multitasking centers (TSUGAMI's core strength)

**Consolidation Trends**: Tier-1 consolidation evident; larger players (DMG Mori ¥436B market cap, Okuma ¥213B) acquiring specialty manufacturers. Mid-size specialists like TSUGAMI (¥90B) increasingly acquisition targets but also demonstrating standalone viability through organic growth.

**Pricing Power**: Improving as supply constraints normalize; customers willing to accept price increases for delivery certainty. TSUGAMI benefiting from 2-3 month lead times (vs. historical 1 month average).

**Market Size**: 
- Japan machine tools market: ¥14B (2024), 6.5% CAGR through 2030 (Grand View Research)
- Global precision machining: $107B (2023), 7.8% CAGR through 2030
- TSUGAMI addressable market estimated ¥30-40B globally (based on product specialization in automatic lathes/multitasking centers)

**Cycle Stage**: Mid-expansion; orders remain robust through 2025, with potential normalization 2026-2027 if macro headwinds materialize. Current cycle has 12-18 months of runway.

### 4.2 Macroeconomic Sensitivities

**Interest Rates**: 
- TSUGAMI relatively insensitive (minimal floating-rate debt, strong cash position)
- Customer capex budgets moderately sensitive to rates; rise in rates (BoJ tightening) could dampen customer orders 2025-2026
- Current BoJ policy (soft stance) supportive; future tightening risk moderate

**Energy Prices**: 
- Moderate exposure; factory electricity/gas costs represent ~3-5% of COGS
- Significant pass-through capability to customers historically
- Current global energy prices benign; no material impact expected

**Trade Flows & Tariffs**: 
- **HIGH SENSITIVITY**: China tariff escalation (US/EU tariffs on China) could redirect customer orders away from China-based TSUGAMI operations, OR reduce end-customer demand if Chinese manufacturers lose competitiveness
- FY2025-FY2026 tariff environment heightened risk (US Trump administration tariff rhetoric, potential 10-20% China tariffs)
- Mitigation: India/Japan expansion reduces China tariff exposure longer-term, but near-term revenue concentration risk

**Currency Risk**: 
- ¥/$ sensitivity: Yen depreciation (1% weaker ¥ = ~0.5% boost to exports in ¥ terms) benefited TSUGAMI FY2023-FY2024
- Current ¥ at 140-150 per $ vs. historical 100-110; ¥ weakening cycle may be over, creating headwind if ¥ strengthens
- China ¥ (RMB) stability not a major concern; TSUGAMI China operations pricing in RMB
- Impact: Moderate (2-3% annual earnings sensitivity per 5% ¥ move)

**China Economic Slowdown**: 
- **CRITICAL MACRO VARIABLE**: 84% revenue from China operations
- China stimulus measures (recent government packages) supporting demand through 2025, but longer-term growth (beyond FY2026) uncertain
- EV adoption in China slowing (market saturation); machinery demand may decelerate accordingly
- Semiconductor fab expansion in China moderating (geopolitical constraints on US chip equipment exports affecting customer capex planning)
- **Probability of China recession/slowdown**: Medium (35-40% likelihood of China GDP growth <3% in 2026-2027, vs. current 5% consensus); if realized, TSUGAMI revenue headwind 10-15%

### 4.3 Competitive Positioning & Moat Assessment

**TSUGAMI's Competitive Advantages:**
1. **Product Specialization**: Market leader in CNC automatic lathes, particularly Swiss-type and multitasking variants; >40 years R&D accumulation
2. **Cost Competitiveness**: Japan-based manufacturing + China production network provides scale advantage; labor productivity superior to larger players (DMG Mori, Okuma) due to niche focus
3. **Customer Relationships**: Strong OEM relationships in semiconduct, automotive, aerospace; switching costs moderate-to-high (2-3 year sales/support lock-in)
4. **Quality Reputation**: Japanese manufacturing pedigree; machines deliver 0.1-0.5 micron tolerance; premium positioning justified

**Moat Durability Assessment: STABLE TO SLIGHTLY WIDENING**
- Moat Type: **Intangible (brand, R&D, customer relationships)** and **Scale** (production network leverage)
- Strength: Moderate-to-strong; defensible but not impregnable
- Trend: **Widening slightly** as:
  - China production network becomes increasingly difficult to replicate (localized supply chain, customer proximity)
  - India facility creates additional geographic moat
  - Multitasking machine R&D leads (5-axis B-axis capability) ahead of competitors
- Erosion Risks:
  - Larger players (DMG Mori, Okuma) could bundle TSUGAMI-type products with systems solutions
  - Chinese local competitors (Huazhong CNC, Dalian Machine Tool) improving capability (though still quality gap remains)
  - AI/automated design tools potentially commoditizing specialized machine design
- **Verdict**: Moat **stable**, unlikely to widen significantly, but defensible through 2027-2028
