**Executive summary** (<150 words)

SBM Offshore N.V. (SBMO.AS) is a leading FPSO designer, builder, and operator with a resilient lease-and-operate model and strong Turnkey execution. In 2025, the company delivered Directional revenue of US$5.1 bn (above guidance) and EBITDA of US$1.7 bn despite IFRS revenue of US$5.9 bn and net profit of US$922 m. Pro-forma Directional backlog stands at US$31.1 bn with US$8.4 bn net cash backlog, supporting minimum US$2.1 bn cash returns through 2031. Three major FPSOs started production in 2025, adding ~655 kbpd capacity. 2026 guidance (Directional revenue ~US$6.5 bn, EBITDA ~US$1.8 bn) reflects continued growth. At ~36.9 EUR (market cap ~EUR 6.14 bn, EV ~EUR 13 bn), the stock trades at 8.2x trailing P/E and ~7–9x EV/EBITDA—compelling versus cash-flow visibility and 9% cash-return yield. Short/medium-term value thesis: undervalued cash engine with visible backlog, rising returns, and FPSO demand tailwinds. **Buy** with high conviction; target 48–55 EUR (30–50% upside).
