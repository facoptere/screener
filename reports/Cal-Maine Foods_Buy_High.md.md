**Executive Summary**
Cal-Maine Foods (CALM), the U.S.'s largest shell egg producer (~20% share), posted FY2025 record \$4.26B revenue (+36% YoY) and \$1.22B net income amid HPAI-driven prices, with H1 FY2026 \$1.69B sales (-3%) and \$302M net (-18%) resilient via specialty (40% shell mix) and prepared foods (9% total sales). Q2 FY2026 GP margin 27%, op 16% despite -27% prices; zero debt, \$1.1B cash funds expansions/dividends/buybacks. At \$92 (Jan 28, 2026), 4x P/E undervalues 28.5% 5-yr ROIC, 30% prepared growth target; HPAI persistence favors scale. Buy for 120%+ upside to \$200-267. (138 words)[^1][^2][^3][^4]
