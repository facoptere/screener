**SPORTSFIELD CO LTD_Buy_High**

**Executive summary** (<150 words)

Sportsfield Co., Ltd. (7080.T) operates a high-margin, asset-light niche in sports personnel recruitment: matching active athletes (new graduates and mid-career/retired pros) with corporate employers seeking sports-minded talent. FY2025 (ended Dec 2025) delivered robust 24.4% revenue growth to ¥4.785 bn, 23.4% operating profit growth to ¥1.079 bn, and 31.5% net profit growth to ¥784 mn, with gross margins stable at ~90.5%. At ¥900 (close 3 Apr 2026), the stock trades at 8.5× FY2025 EPS and ~6.5× EV/EBITDA with a ~15% FCF yield. Strong balance sheet (net cash position), 37% ROE, founder-aligned ownership (~21.6%), and upward-revised mid-term plan signal continued 20-30% growth runway. Short/medium-term value thesis is compelling: undervalued growth compounder with durable niche moat. **Recommendation: Buy.**
