Management raised 2028 targets while guiding 2026 to more moderate but still profitable growth. At ~CHF 157 (10 Apr 2026), the stock trades at ~16.2x trailing EPS, ~6.0x EV/EBITDA and ~9% FCF yield — compelling for a high-moat, cash-generative niche leader with visible digital tailwinds. **Buy** recommendation with High conviction for <2-year horizon.
