# FinVolution Group_Buy_High


***

## Executive Summary

FinVolution Group (NYSE: FINV), China's leading online consumer finance platform with expanding international operations in Indonesia and the Philippines, trades at deeply depressed multiples despite robust cash flow generation and accelerating international growth. LTM revenue stands at US\$1.93B, net income US\$390M (EPS \$1.49), with P/E 4.4x, EV/EBITDA ~1.6x, and FCF yield ~15-20%. Management tone has shifted from defensive to optimistic across 2025 quarters, emphasizing capital-light international expansion and regulatory tailwinds. DCF fair value implies US\$12.5–15.0/share (~100-125% upside from \$6.56). Buy with high conviction for 12-18 months, supported by capital returns, moat widening via global diversification, and sector consolidation.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5]

## 1. Fundamental Analysis

### Revenue growth, gross margin, operating margin, net margin trends

- Revenue: LTM US\$1.93B; 2024 full-year US\$1.79B; Q4 2024 US\$474M; Q1 2025 US\$480M (+10% YoY); Q2 2025 ~US\$500M (+13% YoY); Q3 2025 US\$490M (+6.4% YoY). CAGR ~5-6% over 3 years, accelerating to 10-15% guidance for 2025.[^6][^7][^8][^3][^9][^10]
- Gross margin: Stable ~60-65%, reflecting capital-light shift from risk-bearing loans.[^3]
- Operating margin: Non-GAAP ~25% in 2025 (up from low-20% in 2023), driven by international leverage.[^9]
- Net margin: ~20% LTM, ROE 18.33%; Q1 2025 net income US\$103M (+38.7% YoY). Trends: Improving via scale and mix shift.[^2][^7]


### Regional performance breakdown

- China mainland: Core (~75% revenue), transaction volume +6.1% YoY 2024 to RMB206B, outstanding loans +6.1% to RMB71.5B; pivot to facilitation/guarantee structures.[^11][^9]
- International (Indonesia, Philippines): ~25% revenue Q3 2025 (+37.4% YoY), transaction volume +33-39% YoY, loans +40-46% YoY. Acceleration signals diversification success.[^4][^9]


### Free cash flow evolution and FCF yield

- 2024 FCF est. US\$280-320M (op cash strong, low capex); Q1 2025 op cash RMB522M (+145% YoY). Cumulative capital returns US\$813M since 2018 (~49% of 2024 net profit).[^12][^11]
- FCF yield: 15-20% at \$1.51B mkt cap (252.97M shares). Evolution: Strengthening with international cash contribution.[^2]

## 4. Sector and Macro View

### Sector overview

China consumer fintech in consolidation post-regulation; pricing power limited but leaders gaining share via compliance/tech moats. Cycle: Recovery stage, low penetration (<10% digital credit). Consolidation trends favor scale players like FINV.[^17]

### Key macroeconomic sensitivities

Interest rates (higher hurts borrowing); CNY/USD (stronger CNY aids repatriation); trade flows indirect via consumer confidence; energy minimal.

### Company’s competitive positioning and moat

Top moat via data/AI underwriting, borrower base; **widening** with intl scale and capital-light pivot.
