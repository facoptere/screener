**Executive Summary** (<150 words)

BlueNord ASA (BNOR.OL) is a pure-play Danish North Sea E&P operator with 36.8% non-operated interest in the Danish Underground Consortium (DUC). Following the successful Tyra restart, 2025 full-year production reached a record 37.3 mboepd, driving revenue to USD 1,030m (+47% YoY) and adjusted EBITDA to USD 566m. The company has entered a harvesting phase with capex collapsing to USD 40-50m in 2026 and a disciplined 50-70% payout policy delivering >USD 500m in distributions in 2025 alone. Net debt is declining, liquidity robust at ~USD 493m post-RBL refinance, and unit costs have hit steady-state guidance of ~USD 13/boe. At current levels the stock trades at an attractive EV/EBITDA ~4.9x and offers a double-digit FCF yield underpinned by visible production growth into H1 2026. Short/medium-term value thesis intact: high and growing cash returns with minimal reinvestment risk.
