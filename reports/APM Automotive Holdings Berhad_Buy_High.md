# APM Automotive Holdings Berhad_Buy_High


***

## Executive Summary

APM Automotive Holdings Berhad manufactures automotive components, primarily suspension systems, interiors, and plastics, serving OEMs in Malaysia, Indonesia, and exports. FY2024 revenue grew 7.9% to RM2.08B with PBT up 41.8% to RM153.5M, driven by domestic demand and new models; Q3'2025 revenue flat at RM545.5M amid export softness. Trading at ~MYR3.28 (P/E 7.5x), undervalued vs peers (avg 12.6x) and DCF fair value ~MYR10. Strong FCF position, insider ownership 26%, and robust balance sheet support short/medium-term value upside despite auto cycle risks. Bullish verdict with high conviction buy targeting 50-100% upside in <2 years.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]

## 1. Fundamental Analysis

### Revenue growth, gross margin, operating margin, net margin trends

- FY2024 revenue RM2.08B (+7.9% YoY), Q4'2024 RM590M (+25.4% YoY); 9M'2025 RM1.54B (+3.3% YoY), Q3'2025 RM545.5M (+0.1% YoY).[^2][^3][^7]
- Net margin FY2024 ~5.6% (PBT margin ~7.4%), Q3'2025 4.95%; improved YoY from favorable mix, lower raw materials, but Q3 pressured by competition.[^8][^2]
- Operating margin trends stable ~6-7%, with Q4'2024 PBT margin uplift to 9.3%.[^9]

## 4. Sector \& Macro View

- Competitive positioning: Leader in leaf springs/seats; moat stable via OEM relationships/tooling investments.[^11]
