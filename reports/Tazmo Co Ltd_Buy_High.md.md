# Tazmo Co Ltd_Buy_High.md

**Executive Summary**
Tazmo Co., Ltd. (6266.T), a manufacturer of semiconductor processing equipment, precision molds, and surface treatment gear, exhibits robust profitability with decelerating but positive revenue growth. Strong FCF generation (¥9.3B in FY2025) supports a DCF fair value of ¥3,200-3,500, implying 40-60% upside from current levels (~¥2,200 as of Feb 27, 2026). Management tone remains optimistic amid semi cap cyclicality; recommend **Buy** with high conviction for short/medium-term value.[^1][^2][^3][^4][^5]

## 1. Fundamental Analysis

### Revenue Growth, Margin Trends

- Revenue grew at 27% YoY in FY2024 to ¥35.9B, slowed to 1.2% in FY2025 to ¥35.4B, reflecting semi cap normalization post-boom.[^6][^4]
- Gross margin contracted from 33% (FY2024) to 30.4% (FY2025) due to mix shift and cost pressures; operating margin dipped from 16.5% to 13.5%; net margin from 11.8% to 10%.[^4][^5]
- Quarterly trends: Q1 FY2025 +27% YoY (¥7.5B), H1 flat at ¥16.8B (-3%), 9M +10% (¥27.1B).[^2][^3][^1]


### Regional Performance Breakdown

- Primarily Japan-based, with export reliance via Process Equipment (semi/cleaning/coater ~77% of FY2025 revenue: ¥27.5B); Surface Treatment (19%, ¥6.8B); Molds (3%, ¥1.2B).[^4]
- No explicit regional split in reports, but FX gains/losses indicate overseas exposure (e.g., JPY weakening aided FY2024).[^6]
- Semi equipment sales surged semiconductor ¥17.2B (FY2025, up from ¥12.3B), conveying ¥7.7B, cleaning down sharply.[^4]


### Free Cash Flow Evolution and FCF Yield

- FCF robust: FY2024 ~¥5.8B (op CF ¥7.5B less capex ¥1.7B), FY2025 ~¥6.2B (op CF ¥9.3B less capex ~¥3.2B implied).[^6][^4]
- Cumulative H1 FY2025 op CF ¥4.4B; 9M trends positive.[^2]
- Est. market cap ~¥32B (14.7M shares * ¥2,200); FCF yield ~19% trailing, attractive for value.[^7][^4]
