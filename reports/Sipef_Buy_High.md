**4. Sector and Macro View**

**4–6 line sector overview**

Palm-oil sector is in late-cycle recovery (2024-25 Indonesian production rebound after aging palms). Pricing power limited by vegetable-oil substitution but sustainable/traceable segment commands 5-10% premium. Consolidation ongoing among majors; smaller integrated planters like SIPEF gaining relative share via certifications. EU EUDR and RED II favour compliant producers.

**Geopolitical sensitivities**

- Strait of Hormuz / Red Sea: minimal direct impact (CPO shipped via Pacific/Indian Ocean routes).
- Indonesia export levies (now 12.5%) already priced in; PNG operations insulated.

**Key macroeconomic sensitivities**

- Energy prices: positive (fertiliser/shipping costs); higher oil supports biodiesel demand.
- Interest rates: low sensitivity (net cash position).
- Trade flows / USD: IDR/PGK depreciation offsets local inflation; strong USD good for USD-denominated sales.
- Currency: ~70% revenue USD-linked vs EUR reporting currency – tailwind.

**Competitive positioning and moat assessment**

Moat widening: 100% RSPO + full traceability (GeoSIPEF), low-contaminant CPO (washing plants), F1 hybrid R&D (target double yields 2029), and EUDR-ready status differentiate vs non-certified peers. Scale in premium markets (EU/UK bananas, specialty palm) + integrated smallholder model further strengthens barriers. Moat stable-to-widening.
