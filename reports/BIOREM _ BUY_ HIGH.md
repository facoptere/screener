**Executive Summary** (<150 words)

Biorem Inc. (TSXV: BRM) is a Canadian clean technology company specializing in biofiltration and multi-stage air emissions control systems for odor, VOC, and HAP abatement, primarily serving municipal, industrial, biogas, and cannabis sectors. FY2025 (ended Dec 31, 2025) delivered record revenue of $42.4M (+13% YoY), gross margin expansion to 30.1% (+~320 bps), operating income of $6.5M, and net income of $4.82M (+58% YoY), with diluted EPS of $0.26. A robust $65.3M order backlog (up from $56.1M) signals strong visibility into 2026-2027. Cash position strengthened significantly, net debt turned negative, and ROE reached ~38%. At ~CAD 3.20-3.30 (as of late April 2026), the stock trades at ~12.6x trailing P/E and offers attractive FCF yield. Short/medium-term value thesis centers on backlog conversion, margin sustainability, and sector tailwinds in environmental compliance, supporting a Buy with High conviction and material upside to a $4.00+ target.
