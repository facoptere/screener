## Executive Summary (<150 words)

Seneca Foods (SENEB) is a leading U.S. processor of canned and frozen vegetables with dominant private-label and contract positions. Fiscal 2026 delivered record results: revenue +5.1% to $1,660M, gross margin expanded sharply to 13.9% (from 9.5%), FIFO diluted EPS $16.59 (record), and robust FCF of ~$180M. The March 2026 acquisition of the Green Giant U.S. frozen business reunites the iconic brand under one owner and meaningfully scales the higher-growth frozen segment. The stock trades at deeply discounted multiples (~9.4x TTM P/E, ~7.0x EV/EBITDA, ~5.9x P/FCF) with a fortress balance sheet (net debt/EBITDA ~1.25x, current ratio 5.1x) following aggressive deleveraging. Strong volume recovery, normalized pack costs, and strategic brand expansion support re-rating potential. Risks center on acquisition integration and agricultural commodity volatility. **Bullish value thesis with attractive risk/reward at current levels.**

**Competitive Positioning & Moat**: Leading U.S. canned vegetable processor (private-label #1, foodservice/export strong). Moat from scale (nationwide facilities), long-term customer contracts, and now Green Giant brand ownership. **Widening** post-acquisition: frozen capabilities expanded, iconic brand consolidated, barriers to entry in contract manufacturing reinforced.
